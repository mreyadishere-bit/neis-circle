What can students do?

Connect with students

Discover other students by interests, grade, or branch, and take part in conversations with a sense of community.

Explore projects and ideas

Share learning experiences, creative work, questions, and useful resources with fellow students.

Read and write articles

Find student-written articles and contribute thoughtful writing across English and Arabic.

Discover opportunities

Explore educational activities, events, competitions, and other opportunities shared in the community.

How to join

Open NEIS Circle, continue with Google, and complete your own student profile. Community areas are available to signed-in members.

Privacy matters. Member profiles, private messages, and student-specific content are not published on this public information page. Google sign-in is used to authenticate members.

Frequently asked questions

Is NEIS Circle a public social network?

No. NEIS Circle has a public introduction, but the main student community requires sign-in.

Can I use the platform in Arabic?

Yes. NEIS Circle provides an English and Arabic interface and supports bilingual student content.

Is this an official school website?

NEIS Circle is a student community project, not the official admissions or administration website of a school.
